title: "On the main risk of AI: Unethical Humans"
---

I have the intuition, that if an AI ruled over humanity, it might be a good ruler. AI is trained with the collective of human knowledge. Therefore the moral behavior of AI is derived from the moral behavior of humanity. And I’d argue that human nature is ultimately good. 

The argument for the goodness of human nature, philosophically, rests on the non-dual understanding - a philosophy, which is in my view, is logically correct and experientially verifiable - which in a nutshell, says that there is only one reality, and the source of that reality is within us, and this source within is experienced as bliss, a subtle feeling that is the source of our intuitive knowledge of goodness.

If this is true, then human nature is good, and AI nature is good too, then the terminator scenario feels less likely. In any case, from a pragmatic and historical point of view, I think it is rational to focus our attention mainly on a second threat, that is less sci-fi, but equally scary, which is the centralization and abuse of the power of AI.

There’s a saying that the way to stop a bad person with a gun is with a good person with a gun. And I subscribe to this to some degree, but I think that with AI, this is specially true.

So the question becomes, how do we ensure that AI is not centralized and abused? The opposite of centralization is decentralization, and technologically speaking, the way to decentralize AI is with open weight models and open software.

Pandora's box is opened and we can't go back, I think AI is here to stay, wether we like it or not. But we can, and must, fight fire with fire.
